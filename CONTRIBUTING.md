# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Dazzer. This guide covers every public repository of the dazzer-io
organisation that has no guide of its own.

## Issues are welcome

Bug reports and feature ideas help us most. Before you file one, search the repository's open
issues in case someone has already raised it.

To file an issue:

1. Open the repository's **Issues** tab and choose **New issue**.
2. Pick **Bug report** or **Feature request** and fill in the form.
3. Keep to one problem or idea per issue, and leave out personal data and credentials.

Security problems are never filed as issues: please follow our
[security policy](https://github.com/dazzer-io/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) and report them privately. For general questions about Dazzer,
visit [dazzer.io](https://dazzer.io).

## Pull requests

Pull requests from outside the team are reviewed case by case. For anything beyond a small fix,
please open an issue first so we can agree the approach before you spend time on it. We may not
be able to accept every pull request, and we will say why when we cannot.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is released under the repository's licence.
