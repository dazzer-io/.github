# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Dazzer and its users safe. This policy covers every public repository
of the dazzer-io organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, never in a public issue, pull request or discussion.

1. Open the repository the problem is in, for example
   [dazzer-io/dazzer-plugin](https://github.com/dazzer-io/dazzer-plugin).
2. Go to its **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Fill in the form and submit it. Only you and the repository's maintainers can see the report.

If you are unsure which repository the problem belongs to, report it on the one you found it in.

## What to include

- What the problem is and what an attacker could do with it.
- The repository, and the version, release or commit you looked at.
- The steps to reproduce it, as short as you can make them, with any proof of concept.
- Where you know it: the file, workflow or setting involved, and any fix you suggest.
- How you would like to be credited, if at all.

Please do not include real people's personal data or anyone's credentials in a report.

## What happens next

- **Within 5 business days** we acknowledge your report.
- **Within 10 business days** we send a status update: whether we could reproduce the problem,
  how serious we judge it, and what we plan to do. We keep you updated until it is resolved.
- When a fix is ready, we release it and publish a security advisory on the repository, crediting
  you if you wish.

## Coordinated disclosure

We ask you to give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before you share it with anyone else, and
we will agree the publication date with you. Please do not access, change or delete data that is
not yours, disrupt our services, or test against other people's accounts. Research done in good
faith under this policy is welcome.
